# Met Office data attribution

**Source:** Met Office historical monthly meteorological station data.

**Source page:**
https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/research/climate/maps-and-data/historic-station-data

**Licence:** UK Open Government Licence, as identified by the official dataset
catalogue:
https://www.data.gov.uk/dataset/17ba3bbe-0e98-4a8c-9937-bd1d50fdc3c5/historic-monthly-meteorological-station-data

> Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government
> Licence v3.0.

The licence text is available at:
https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/

This repository preserves the CSV snapshot used for the original analysis so
that its recorded results remain reproducible. The individual station series
include observations marked provisional. Users who require the latest data
should retrieve updated files from the Met Office source page.
